Put the ARM64 Windows libsndfile here (see ../README.md for full steps):

PREFERRED - one self-contained DLL (deps linked in statically):
  libsndfile.dll      (single file; rename from the mixed-triplet sndfile.dll)
  LICENSE-*.txt       (libsndfile LGPL + each bundled dependency's license)

ALTERNATIVE - plain dynamic build:
  libsndfile.dll      (rename from vcpkg's sndfile.dll)
  <dependency DLLs>   (FLAC.dll, ogg.dll, vorbis.dll, opus.dll, mpg123.dll, ...)
  LICENSE-*.txt

IMPORTANT (security): the loader only loads a libsndfile whose SHA-256 is pinned
in audio.py (_SNDFILE_SHA256). After placing libsndfile.dll, compute its hash:
  (Get-FileHash .\libsndfile.dll -Algorithm SHA256).Hash.ToLower()
and add it to _SNDFILE_SHA256 (e.g. 'windows-arm64': '<hex>'). An unpinned or
swapped DLL here is ignored.

Once the file is here AND its hash is pinned, it auto-loads on ARM64 Windows
(source runs) and is bundled into ARM64 builds. Delete this file once done.
